How Working Days Are Calculated

Calculating working days between two dates requires separating regular business operations from non-working days. While a full calendar year consists of 365 days (or 366 in a leap year), not all days are active business days.

Formula:

Working Days = Total Calendar Days – (Weekend Days + Non-Weekend Public Holidays)

  • Calendar Days vs Working Days: Calendar days include every day from Monday to Sunday. Working days (or business days) exclude non-working weekend days and official public holidays.
  • Leap Years: In leap years (such as 2024 or 2028), February contains 29 days, which can adjust the annual working day total depending on where February 29 falls in the week.
  • Start & End Date Inclusivity: Standard calculations measure the duration interval between two dates. Toggling "Include End Date" adds 1 additional day if the target end date lands on a valid working day.
